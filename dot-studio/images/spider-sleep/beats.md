# spider-sleep — beat sheet

Script: scripts/jumping-spider-sleep.md (approved). One edit for speech: "It resembles our REM sleep" -> "It looks like
our REM sleep" (same meaning, same word count).

Setting: night garden. A twig crosses the upper frame with a small silk web hammock under it; a fuzzy cartoon jumping
spider hangs upside down beneath the web. Dot stands on the grass below, a small film camera on a tripod beside him.
Sky = code gradient + stars + moon. One continuous camera, one punch-in for the spider close-up, pull back at the end.
