## Voiceover

Why did crocodiles survive the asteroid that wiped out the dinosaurs? A new study from University College London looked at over seven thousand fossils. For two hundred and fifty million years, crocodile relatives mostly stuck to warm places. After the impact, the tropics stayed warm enough for them, while dinosaurs lost far more of the climate they needed. Being cold-blooded, small, and at home in water probably helped too.
