# Why do your fingers go wrinkly in the bath?

- **Content type:** Fun fact / interesting question (human body, everyday mystery)
- **Idea:** Bath wrinkles aren't water soaking in: nerves make the fingertip blood vessels narrow and the skin gets pulled into folds (a finger with a damaged nerve stays smooth); whether it helps grip is still debated.
- **Word count:** 70
- **Status:** Option, awaiting approval (2026-11-03)

## Voiceover

Why do your fingers go wrinkly in the bath? It's not water soaking in. It's your nerves. They tell tiny blood vessels in your fingertips to narrow, and the skin on top gets pulled into folds. Doctors use this as a nerve test: a finger with a damaged nerve stays smooth. As for why, it's still debated. Some studies say wrinkles help us grip wet things. Others found no difference.
