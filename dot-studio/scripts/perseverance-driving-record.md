# Who has driven farthest on another world?

- **Content type:** Current affairs (space)
- **Why now:** NASA announced on 13 October 2026 that Perseverance passed Opportunity's off-world driving record on 11 October 2026 (sol 2,006).
- **Idea:** Perseverance beat Opportunity's 15-year driving record in about five and a half years because it plans most of its own route.
- **Word count:** 69
- **Status:** Option, awaiting approval (2026-11-02)

## Voiceover

What's the farthest any vehicle has driven on another world? Until last month, NASA's Opportunity held the record: about forty-five kilometres, in almost fifteen years. On October 11th, Perseverance beat it in five and a half. It mostly drives itself. Mars is too far to steer live, so it maps the ground and picks its own path. Opportunity did that for five percent of its trip. Perseverance, over ninety.
