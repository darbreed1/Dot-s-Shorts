# Why did Chicago lift its buildings?

- **Content type:** Niche (engineering / history)
- **Idea:** In the 1850s-60s, Chicago was too low for sewers to drain, so it raised its streets and jacked whole buildings up to match, sometimes with businesses still open.
- **Word count:** 68
- **Status:** Approved and produced 2026-11-02 (composition RaisingChicago, out/raising-chicago-4K.mp4)

## Voiceover

In the 1860s, Chicago lifted whole buildings with people still inside. The city sat on a swamp, barely above the lake. Sewers need a downhill slope. There wasn't one. So Chicago raised its streets by several feet, and buildings had to rise to match. In 1860, six hundred men turned six thousand jackscrews and lifted a row of Lake Street shops in five days. The shops stayed open.
