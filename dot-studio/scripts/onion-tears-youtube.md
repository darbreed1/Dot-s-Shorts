## Title
Why Do Onions Make You Cry? 🧅😢

## Description
Onions make you cry because they're fighting back.

An onion keeps two ingredients stored apart inside its cells. When a knife breaks those cells, the ingredients mix and a special enzyme turns them into an irritating gas. The gas drifts up to your eyes, nerves there feel the sting, and your tears wash it away. Scientists think it's probably the onion's way of putting off hungry animals.

Want fewer tears? Cornell researchers filmed onions being cut with high-speed cameras and found that a sharp knife, used slowly, sprays far less mist than a dull blade or a fast chop.
