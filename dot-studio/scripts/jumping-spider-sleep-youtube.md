## Title
Do Spiders Dream? 🕷️💤

## Description
Spiders don't just sleep. A new study suggests they sleep in stages, a bit like we do.

Researchers at the University of Bonn filmed Australian jumping spiders (Portia fimbriata) through the night and saw two distinct stages. In the first, the spiders hung upside down beneath their webs and slowly drooped. In the second, a REM-like stage, their legs curled, their eyes moved and their bodies twitched. Spiders in that stage were the slowest to react to a sound, and some didn't react at all.

So do spiders dream? Nobody knows yet. The team recorded behaviour only, not brain activity, so they can't say what the spiders experience.

Study: Geiger et al., "Behavioral evidence for two-stage sleep in spiders," PNAS (October 2026)
