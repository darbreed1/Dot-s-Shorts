# Is the world ending on November 13th?

- **Content type:** Trending / viral (maths and history)
- **Why now:** The "Doomsday: Friday, 13 November 2026" claim has been recirculating since early October 2026 (The Conversation/Gizmodo, 1 Oct; The New Daily, 13 Oct) and will peak on 13 November 2026.
- **Idea:** The viral doomsday date comes from a real 1960 Science paper whose population curve hit infinity in 2026; it was a warning about runaway growth, and real numbers have since fallen behind the curve.

## Voiceover

Is the world ending on Friday, November 13th, 2026? That date comes from a real 1960 paper in the journal Science. Three researchers fitted two thousand years of population estimates to one curve. Growth kept speeding up, so their curve hit infinite people on that exact Friday. It was a warning, not a prophecy. And it didn't hold. Families got smaller, and real numbers fell behind the curve.
