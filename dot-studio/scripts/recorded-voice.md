# Why does your voice sound weird on a recording?

- **Content type:** Fun fact / everyday mystery (human body, sound)
- **Idea:** You hear your own voice through the air and through the bones of your head, which boosts the low tones; a microphone only catches the air, so a recording is your voice without that extra bass, roughly as others hear it.
- **Word count:** 70
- **Status:** Option, awaiting approval (2026-11-03)

## Voiceover

Why does your voice sound weird on a recording? Because you've never heard it the way others do. When you talk, sound reaches your inner ear two ways: through the air, and through the bones of your head. That bone route boosts the deep, low tones. A microphone only catches the air. So the recording is your voice without the extra bass, which is roughly how everyone else hears you.
