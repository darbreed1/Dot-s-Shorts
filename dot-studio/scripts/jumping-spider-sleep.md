# Do spiders sleep in stages?

- **Content type:** Current affairs (animals / biology)
- **Why now:** University of Bonn study published in PNAS on 26 October 2026, covered widely 26–27 October 2026.
- **Idea:** A new study filmed jumping spiders through the night and found two sleep stages, one resembling our REM sleep, but whether spiders dream is still unknown.
- **Word count:** 70

## Voiceover

Spiders don't just sleep. They seem to sleep in stages, like we do. Scientists in Bonn filmed Australian jumping spiders all night. First, they hung upside down under their webs and drooped. Then came a second stage: legs curling, eyes moving, small twitches. It looks like our REM sleep, when most vivid dreams happen. Spiders in this stage were the hardest to wake. So do they dream? Nobody can tell yet.
