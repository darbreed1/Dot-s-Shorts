## Title
Why Did Crocodiles Survive the Asteroid That Killed the Dinosaurs? 🐊☄️

## Description
Why did crocodiles survive the asteroid that wiped out the dinosaurs? A new study points to where they lived.

Researchers at University College London looked at more than 7,000 fossils and records from over 400 living and extinct species of crocodiles and their relatives, and compared them with reconstructions of Earth's climate over 245 million years. They found that for most of that time crocodile relatives stuck to warm places: they spread out when the planet was hot and pulled back toward the tropics when it cooled.

After the asteroid struck 66 million years ago, the team's climate models suggest the tropics stayed warm enough for crocodiles, while earlier modelling by the same group found the climate that dinosaurs needed shrank far more. The researchers say climate wasn't the whole story: being cold-blooded (so needing less food), fairly small, living partly in water and eating almost anything probably helped too.
