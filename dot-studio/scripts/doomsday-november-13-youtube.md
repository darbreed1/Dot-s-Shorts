## Title
Is the World Ending on Friday, November 13th, 2026? 📅🌍

## Description
Is the world ending on Friday, November 13th, 2026? The date going around online is real, but it isn't a prophecy.

It comes from a 1960 paper in the journal Science by Heinz von Foerster, Patricia Mora and Lawrence Amiot at the University of Illinois, titled "Doomsday: Friday, 13 November, A.D. 2026". They fitted about 2,000 years of world population estimates to a single curve. Population hadn't just been growing, it had been growing faster and faster, and if that kept up, their formula reached infinite people on Friday 13 November 2026.

The eye-catching date was a way to make a serious point: growth like that can't go on. And it didn't. Soon after the paper came out, the world's population growth rate peaked (in the early 1960s, at a little over 2% a year) and has been falling since. Families got smaller, and from the early 1990s real population numbers fell behind the 1960 curve.

So the paper is now a classic warning about stretching a trend too far into the future, not a doomsday date.
