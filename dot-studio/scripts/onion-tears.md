# Why do onions make you cry?

- **Content type:** Fun fact / interesting question (food science)
- **Idea:** Cutting an onion mixes two separately stored ingredients that an enzyme turns into an eye-stinging gas, probably a defence against animals, and a Cornell study found a sharp knife used slowly sprays less of it.
- **Word count:** 68

## Voiceover

Why do onions make you cry? Because they're fighting back. An onion stores two ingredients apart. Cut it, and they mix. A special enzyme turns them into an irritating gas that drifts up to your eyes. Nerves there feel the sting, so tears wash it away. It's probably how onions put off hungry animals. Want fewer tears? Cornell researchers found that a sharp knife, used slowly, sprays less mist.
