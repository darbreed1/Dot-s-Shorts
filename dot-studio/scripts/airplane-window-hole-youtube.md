## Title
Why Do Airplane Windows Have a Tiny Hole? ✈️🪟

## Description
Why is there a tiny hole in airplane windows? It's on purpose.

Each passenger window has three panes. The thin inner one is just a scratch pane. The tiny "breather" or "bleed" hole is drilled through the middle pane. Up at cruising height, the air inside the cabin pushes hard against the much thinner air outside. The hole lets that cabin pressure into the gap between the middle and outer panes, so the outer pane carries the load on its own, and the middle pane stays ready as a backup in case the outer one ever fails.

The hole also lets moisture out of the gap, so your window doesn't fog up (on long flights you may still see a little frost around it).
